# 0005. Two-level record model: source records plus decision records

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Status:** Accepted

## Options considered
One record per source; one record per decision; both, linked

## Decision and reasoning
A single paper often contains two or three distinct product decisions, and the co-writer matches at the decision level.

## Revisit if
Extraction can't split decisions reliably in the pilot.
