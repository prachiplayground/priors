# 0010. Differentiate on decision-level structure and evals, not on being a library

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Status:** Accepted

## Options considered
Compete as a broader case-study library; differentiate on decision-level structure, evidence pointers, problem-shape matching, the co-writer and published evals

## Decision and reasoning
A landscape scan found open case-study libraries (e.g. Product Case Studies, ~250 entries with keyword-search MCP), ML/LLM case-study databases (Evidently AI, ZenML) and AI PRD writers (ChatPRD and others). Each piece exists on its own; the combination doesn't. Existing libraries store one record per article and support keyword search, not questions across decisions. priors' edge is the schema depth, the evidence pointers and measured quality, which are also the hardest parts to copy.

## Revisit if
An existing library adds decision-level structured fields, or a PRD writer adds a sourced precedent corpus.
