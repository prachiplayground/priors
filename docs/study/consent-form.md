# Consent form

**Study:** Does sparring with real product case studies help PMs make better decisions?
**Researcher:** Prachi Mehta (independent project; not affiliated with any company)
**Length:** about 60 minutes, online

## What you'll do

You'll describe how you usually research product decisions, frame a product decision with your usual tools, then revisit it using a prototype called priors, and share your feedback. You can use your own decision or a prepared scenario. **Please don't share confidential information about your employer.** The prepared scenario is there so you never need to.

## Recording

The session is recorded (screen and audio, and video only if you choose). Recordings are stored privately, are never posted to the project's public repository, and are deleted 12 months after the study ends unless you agree below to publication.

## How your input is used

Your notes, ratings and quotes are anonymized (you'll be "P1", "P2" and so on, with no company name) and used to improve the prototype and in a public write-up of the study.

## Your choices

Please mark each one. Saying no to any of them doesn't affect your participation.

- [ ] I agree to take part and to be recorded for research purposes.
- [ ] I agree to anonymized quotes from my session being published.
- [ ] I agree to short clips of my **screen and voice** being published, after I've reviewed them.
- [ ] I agree to short clips showing **my face** being published, after I've reviewed them.
- [ ] I'm happy to be named (with my role) in the write-up, rather than anonymized.

## Your rights

- You can skip any question, pause, or stop at any time.
- You can withdraw afterwards and have your data deleted. Just email the researcher.
- You'll see any quote or clip before it's published and can veto it.
